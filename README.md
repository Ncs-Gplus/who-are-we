# who-are-we
## what is Ncs?

 *To understand Ncs, we look back to the 1980s when software started getting locked away. We stand right in the middle of open-source philosophies. We aren't against money; it's a necessary emulator for trade, and developers need to make a living. However, we are strictly against giant corporations closing source code, monopolizing technology, and weaponizing software for digital control. Ncs is neither a utopian dream nor a corporate tool—it’s just keeping software open and realistic(ncs means not closed source)

*The software industry is more divided than ever. Open-source communities often waste their immense collective power on internal arguments over licenses, naming, and dogmas. While we fight amongst ourselves, giant corporations easily monopolize technology and lock away human knowledge.Ncs exists to end these borders.We are not bound by dogmatic rules or specific licenses. Our vision is simple, realistic, and universal:We believe developers deserve to make a living.We believe code must remain transparent and accessible.If it is not closed source, it belongs to the same front.No matter which open-source license you choose or which philosophy you follow—if your software is free from corporate walls at the end of the day, you are already a part of Ncs.Let's stop dividing. Let's start uniting.
